Cognitive Architecture

We're working with sparse networks consisting of hyper-neurons, neurons that can connect to any node within the network.

Hyper-connectivity allows for the creation of all the network structures found in grey matter, such as delay or loopback structures, enabling deep cognitive processing.

Performance & Efficiency

Our replacement for stochastic gradient descent is a ramping mechanism designed for analog photonics. By releasing AI from the constraints of differentiable functions, we open up new operational bounds.

Analog photonics offer significantly lower energy consumption, higher speeds, and superior scalability compared to traditional silicon hardware configurations.
