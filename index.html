Our Vision

The Next Evolution of AI

Beyond brute force. A fundamentally different approach to artificial general intelligence.

Biologically Inspired

In a world captivated by the rapid advancements in AI, one challenge remains constant: the immense cost and complexity. We are building a fundamentally different approach.

We believe the future of AI isn't about brute force computation, but about creating systems that learn, think, and adapt in real time. We're not just building AI — we're crafting intelligence that evolves.

Emergence & Adaptation

We are developing novel approaches to Artificial General Intelligence, with a focus on creating highly adaptive robotic systems.

Our biologically-inspired architectures can be implemented without any proprietary software or hardware — yet remain capable of self-organizing into complex, emergent behaviors.

Cognitive Architecture

We work with sparse networks of hyper-neurons — neurons that can connect to any node within the network.

Hyper-connectivity allows the creation of all network structures found in grey matter, such as delay or loopback structures, enabling deep cognitive processing.

Performance & Efficiency

Our replacement for stochastic gradient descent is a ramping mechanism designed for analog photonics. By releasing AI from the constraints of differentiable functions, we open up new operational bounds.

Analog photonics offers significantly lower energy consumption, higher speeds, and superior scalability compared to traditional silicon hardware configurations.
